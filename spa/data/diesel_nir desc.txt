NIR spectra of diesel fuels (Southwest Research Institute, SWRI)

381 samples (with a valid target), 401 spectral variables
(wavelengths 750-1550 nm, 2 nm step).
The 'label' column is the Cetane Number (CN), a regression target.

The source .mat file (PLS_Toolbox DataSet) also contains other
properties: BP50, D4052, FLASH, FREEZE, TOTAL (total aromatics), VISC.

Task: regression (cetane number).

Source:

    https://eigenvector.com/resources/data-sets/
