NIR spectra for mango dry matter content (DMC) prediction

12011 samples, 306 spectral variables (wavelengths 285-1200 nm, 3 nm step).
The 'label' column is the dry matter content (DM, %), a regression target.
An additional 'Tag' column holds the mango cultivar.

Task: regression (dry matter content).

Original publication:

    Anderson, N.T., Walsh, K.B., et al.
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.postharvbio.2020.111202
